DSLR Dynamical Systems : Limits and Recurrence

DSLR is a reading seminar on dynamical systems. Its purpose is to approach Hamiltonian dynamics, as studied in symplectic geometry, from a more dynamical perspective, with a particular focus on understanding the bifurcation behavior of Hamiltonian orbits.
